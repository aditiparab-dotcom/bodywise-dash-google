🔍 Key finding: Demand Gen campaigns are optimising for the wrong goal

The video/conversion tab reveals a critical issue: DemGen campaigns count PDP views, YouTube follow-on views, and ATC events as "conversions" — inflating their reported numbers. When you strip those out and look at BBW Master Purchase only, every DemGen campaign except HairandBody MOF-DropOff collapses to near-zero purchase efficiency. The campaigns that look worst in ROAS are actually even worse than they appear — and those that look good are pulling real purchase volume.

⚠ View-through conversions inflating DemGen ROAS

DemGen Body-Int-02 has 5,367 PDP views but only 67 purchases — a purchase rate of just 1.2%. Yet it reports ROAS of 0.64× including all conversion types. DemGen Hair-LA-02 has 2,348 PDPs but only 26 purchases (1.1%). DemGen Hair-Comp03: 285 PDPs, 22 purchases — but also 137 "YouTube follow-on views" counted as conversions. These view-through events are almost certainly the reason these campaigns show any conversion value at all. Set primary conversion goal to BBW Master Purchase only for all campaigns.

YouTube follow-on views counted as conversions (Apr 13 snapshot)

These are non-purchase "conversions" being credited to campaigns — YouTube follow-on views happen when someone watches a second YouTube video after seeing your ad. They should NOT count as primary conversions.

✓ What makes a top-performing video

Urea Body Lotion (Snake & Dry Skin) — 22.7% VTR, 21.6% watch-to-100%, 1.54× ROAS at ₹69K spend. Highest completion rate in account — the hook is clearly working.

Underarm Pigmentation Kit (Thoughtful Threads) — 156 conversions at ₹325 CAC, 2.51× ROAS. Best CAC among any video with meaningful volume. The transformation angle converts well.

De-Tan SPF 50+ — 2.59× ROAS, 21.2% VTR. Seasonal relevance (summer) driving intent. Scale this creative immediately.

Magnesium Gummies (Anshika Thakur) — 2.42× ROAS, 25.3% VTR, 25.7% W100. Exceptional completion — the only MG video that works. Isolate and scale in its own campaign.

✗ What's failing — and why

Urea Roll-On (Suhasini) — ₹12.9L at 0.77× ROAS. This is your #1 biggest spend video and it's sub-breakeven. VTR only 2.1%, W100 only 2.3% — viewers are skipping immediately. The creative is fatigued or the audience match is wrong.

Urea Roll-On (Anushree) — ₹5.8L at 0.78× ROAS. Same pattern. Both Urea Roll-On videos are failing at scale because DemGen Body-Int-02 is serving them to cold audiences who don't have cracked heel intent.

7% Glycolic Body Stick (Raisha + Hiteshi) — ₹3.8L combined, 0.76–0.87× ROAS. Glycolic campaigns shouldn't run on DemGen at this volume. Switch to retargeting-only or PMax.

Biotin Gummies (Pooja) — ₹3.9L, 0.87× ROAS running only in DG_Hair_Int-Hyd. The Hyderabad geo + this creative isn't converting. Pause this specific pairing.
